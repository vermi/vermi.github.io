About The Course

Applied Cybersecurity is a pilot course, now in its second year, running in the Fall and Spring semesters of the 2026-2027 school year. It is a dual-enrollment course with students from three New Mexico high schools participating alongside NMT students.

The primary objective of this two-semester course is to introduce students to the field of cybersecurity and prepare them for the CompTIA Security+ certification exam (SY0-701).

Course materials include:

  • Textbook: CompTIA Security+ Study Guide: Exam SY0-701 Ninth Edition by Mike Chapple and David Seidl (ISBN: 9781394211418).
  • Virtual Labs: We will be using ACI Skill Labs and LEXX Labs (formerly 30 Bird), both hosted through ACI Learning.
